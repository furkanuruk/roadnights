RoadNights Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

RoadNights ("the app") helps truck drivers log days away from home and estimate IRS per diem. We built it so your records stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Your calendar entries, notes, receipt photos, profile and settings are stored only on your phone (iPhone or Android) and in your own device backups. We do not have servers that receive this data, and there is no account to create. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no location tracking.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat, Inc. to validate purchases and unlock Pro features. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic device information (such as app version and OS). This data is not linked to your name or email and is not used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

IRS rate updates

The app periodically downloads a public file of IRS per diem rates. The request contains no identifiers.

Exports

PDF and CSV reports are created on your device. They leave it only when you choose to share them.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We'll post any changes on this page. RoadNights is published by Latenlukken Studio. Questions: info@latenlukken.com or Support.
