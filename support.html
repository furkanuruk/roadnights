How do I restore my purchase?

Settings → Unlock RoadNights Pro → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RoadNights Pro.

Which rates does the app use?

The IRS special M&IE rates for the transportation industry ($80/day in the continental US, $86/day outside it for FY2027), 75% on partial days, and the 80% limit for drivers under DOT hours of service.

Is this tax advice?

No. RoadNights is a record-keeping tool. Your tax professional decides what applies to you.
